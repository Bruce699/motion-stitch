# UI styling and staging audit

The shared taste is **selective emphasis**, not a uniform rendering style. These films repeatedly simplify the surrounding stage, enlarge a meaningful detail, and then show where it belongs. They disagree productively about hardware, shadows, texture and dimensionality. OpenAI's largely flat editorial graphics are as deliberate as Liquid Glass's optical material studies.

This review visually inspected all five overviews, 22 chronological sheets and full-frame details in each film. The [32 evidence records](styling.json) cover 32 distinct semantic scenes; they are a targeted stylistic sample, not a frame-by-frame census. Times below use the selected local reels. Counts are film coverage first, then distinct semantic scenes within this sample. Multiple shots of the same subject count once. Three films are Apple films, so film coverage and independent brand coverage are different things.

## The useful stylistic choices

### 1. Choose the amount of context for the current claim

- **Realistic device:** Liquid Glass 10–18.72, 125–137.34; iOS 7 43.33–54, 172–183; Yosemite 51–58. The frame and hardware identify scale, orientation and product. They are often cropped closely, rather than obligatorily displayed as a complete centered object.
- **Screen-shaped card:** Airbnb 10–14 and 49–54; Liquid Glass Notes 75.21–91.76. Rounded corners and screen proportions survive, while hardware disappears. Airbnb's status bars do not imply a modeled phone bezel.
- **Unframed interface/component:** iOS 7 79–100; Yosemite 0–21; Airbnb 14–26.5; OpenAI 18–38. The film can discuss a component's construction without pretending it is a physical device.
- **Full-bleed macro crop:** Liquid Glass's opening, iOS 7 Mail typography, Airbnb's letter contour and stitching, OpenAI's large glyphs. Cropping is a form of emphasis. It lets a tiny production detail become the entire visual subject.

The option set is therefore larger than “phone mockup or no phone mockup.” Hardware, a screen-shaped plane, a window, a control, a diagram and a crop are distinct levels of explanation.

### 2. Give backgrounds a specific job

- **Neutral stage:** often holds the outside of a product or an isolated specimen. White UI on white still needs an edge, shadow, outline or other local contrast to stay legible.
- **Content beneath UI:** Liquid Glass's floral/green playback samples and iOS 7's three wallpaper variants let the same interface be compared under different conditions. Yosemite's scenery colors the apparent frosted regions. Airbnb's ocean is a test substrate beneath swatches.
- **Saturated technical field:** Airbnb puts layout on blue and line-icon construction on ochre. The color distinguishes a chapter and provides contrast for pale diagrams. This is a strong, comparatively local choice—not a shared rule across all five films.
- **Photography as composition:** OpenAI 78–92 alternates full bleed, inset and paired crops. The photos are part of the brand language itself; they are not automatically wallpaper, a literal room behind floating UI, or evidence of a 3D environment.

Do not conflate the neutral space outside a phone with the wallpaper inside it. The same frame often combines both roles.

### 3. Use the kind of separation the subject needs

- **Shadow separation:** Airbnb's white feature card at 49–54 has a soft surrounding shadow. Liquid Glass Notes has a diffuse shadow under the UI plane and separation under its menu. Yosemite dock icons show small shadows and rims. These cues support depth without requiring a dark theatrical background.
- **Outline separation:** Airbnb's chips and iOS 7's keypad use thin edges. Their hierarchy does not depend on large blurred shadows.
- **Silhouette separation:** OpenAI's black circles and glyphs, and Yosemite's dark line symbols, need little more than strong figure/ground contrast.
- **Occlusion separation:** OpenAI's closing editorial collage establishes front/back ordering mainly through overlap and crisp rectangular edges. A visible cast shadow is not necessary for stacking to read.
- **Optical edge separation:** Liquid Glass uses bright rims, local distortions and softened content to make transparent surfaces visible. The audit describes the pixels, not an inferred shader, light rig or depth-of-field implementation.

### 4. Let material richness vary by subject

Airbnb's fine outlined chips and highly textured suitcase belong to the same film. The suitcase's leather grain, thread loops and rivets invite inspection; the chips stay simple enough to read as a system. Yosemite similarly mixes flat toolbar glyphs with richly illustrated dock objects. iOS 7's simplification still permits gradient icon fills and translucent Game Center bubbles. “Flat redesign” is not equivalent to “every visual element is flat.”

Liquid Glass makes optical material the headline, so enlarged highlights and thickness are central. OpenAI demonstrates that a polished film can take a different route: crisp vectors, bold type, diagrams and photographic texture, without turning the identity into glossy objects.

### 5. Typography can operate at two scales at once

Airbnb and OpenAI combine large letterform specimens with small labels, coordinates or weight information. iOS 7 places specifications over actual Mail content, and Yosemite turns enlarged Finder lettering back into menu-bar typography. The small technical markings make construction visible; the enlarged form remains the primary image.

That is different from adding explanatory copy to every beauty shot. In the Apple functional scenes, ordinary UI labels usually supply context. In OpenAI's photographic chapter, widely distributed words become part of the composition. These are distinct typographic roles, not one shared prescription.

### 6. Preserve the distinction between proof and flourish

A specimen grid or repeated device array allows comparison. A tactile icon close-up creates appreciation of finish. A feature card and a complete desktop demonstrate practical use. An abstract dot field or editorial collage presents identity range. All appear in the collection, but they do different work. A future scene can choose its role explicitly rather than applying every treatment at once.
