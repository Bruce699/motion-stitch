# Animation principles from the five selected films

The strongest shared pattern is a change in the level of explanation: isolate a subject, inspect its behavior or construction, then reconnect it to a family, interface or broader system. The films disagree about rendering style. Their common ground is how they organize attention.

This audit documents **49 approaches**, with five specialist reviews: **69 semantic scenes, 42 transition events (26 between scenes / 16 internal), 35 camera intervals, 32 styling records and six measured coordinate tracks representing five visual events**. The timing measurements cover three films. Three of the five films are Apple films.

## The films take different routes

| Film | Characteristic approach | Useful contrast |
|---|---|---|
| Liquid Glass | Optical edges, material close-ups, oblique UI planes, background-dependent surfaces; return to recognizable devices. | Material and depth are often the message. A fixed frontal presentation would hide some of that evidence. |
| iOS 7 | White hardware, thin type, icon construction, exploded UI layers and recognizable app behavior. | The simplified interface still contains gradients, atmospheric imagery and dimensional explanations. |
| Yosemite | Icon and type specimens, shape-linked handoffs, frosted windows, desktop and laptop context. | Flat glyphs coexist with rich illustrated icons; scale connects component craft to the whole system. |
| Airbnb | Technical diagrams, rounded UI cards, tactile icons, component families and selective saturated backgrounds. | Fine flat controls and richly textured objects coexist; a modeled phone bezel is not the default carrier. |
| OpenAI | Dots, vector construction, large type, restrained flat geometry, photography and editorial collage. | Scale, silhouette and overlap create variety without requiring a 3D camera or soft shadows. |

## Scenes and transitions are different units

A content scene can contain several cuts. Airbnb’s suitcase outline, leather surface, stitching macro and sticker treatment belong to one sustained object study. OpenAI’s type outlines, filled glyphs and weight specimens form related content groups. The scene map groups the subject; the transition audit separately labels internal changes and handoffs to a new subject.

Of the **26 sampled original handoffs between content scenes**, the primary visible treatments are: four direct cuts, four continuous anchor bridges, four scale bridges, three matched cuts, three directional replacements, three motif resets, two overlapping assemblies, two neutral-field resets and one interface-state bridge. These labels describe the chosen examples, not the proportion of all cuts in the films. The 16 joins introduced while making our recuts are excluded.

A recognizable anchor is supported in **14 of these 26 sampled handoffs, across all five films**. It need not be a literal morph: a circle, location, color or subject identity can survive a cut. Compare this with the four plain cuts in the sample; a cut can be the appropriate handoff when the next idea already reads clearly. [See every transition and its scope](transitions.md).

|---|---|---:|---|

| OpenAI palette circle diameter | 65.733–66.133 s | 81.5% | Another one-frame jump precedes a 400 ms tail. |
| Yosemite red control appearance | 0.033–0.601 s | 87.5% | Fast growth and deceleration; the initial invisible baseline is inferred. |
| Yosemite red control enlargement | 1.068–2.436 s | 94.1% | Most growth arrives early in a 1.368 s interval. |
| Airbnb chip vertical position | 14.833–15.200 s | 43.5% | An S-shaped transfer, not a front-loaded settling tail. |
| Airbnb chip horizontal position | 15.000–15.867 s | 80.0% | Starts later and finishes later than the vertical channel. |

The Airbnb channels start **167 ms apart** and finish **667 ms apart**, shaping a curved path. The two OpenAI discontinuities cover roughly **89–92% of their total growth in one 33 ms frame interval**, followed by residual settling. That is visible evidence for a snap-plus-tail structure; it cannot distinguish a cut from a sub-frame animation.

Seven fixed curve candidates and a flexible cubic Bézier were compared against native-frame pixel measurements, with endpoint sensitivity checks. These are descriptions of screen motion, **not recovered production easing curves or camera rigs**. No numerical easing claims are made for Liquid Glass or iOS 7. [Read the measurement report, plots and caveats](easing.md).

## Camera conclusions

The camera is not visibly moving all the time. All five films include local animation inside a stable-looking frame, frontal reading views, and move-then-hold passages. Some moves inspect a material; others change the level of context. OpenAI’s circle growth and flat layouts should not automatically be described as camera dollies.

Changing relative angles occur, but a rendered film often cannot separate camera orbit from object rotation. Oblique views with depth cues do not establish exact isometric projection. The camera report distinguishes visible direction and framing from an inferred rig, and compares seven original neighboring-shot pairs. [Read the detailed camera audit](camera.md).

## Applying this to Tilt

These are authoring guidelines, not a proposal to turn Tilt into an effect catalogue. For a new sequence:

1. Name the content idea of each scene and the state the viewer should understand at its end.
2. Choose the level of explanation: component, construction, material detail, usable interface or broader family.
3. Choose the view that reveals it: stable frontal, macro crop, oblique inspection, relative rotation or lateral survey.
4. Decide what carries attention into the next scene. Use a cut when no extended relationship needs explaining.
5. Give coordinated channels meaningful timing and a recognizable arrival; verify the actual motion rather than choosing an easing name by habit.
6. Select hardware, surface, background and separation cues for the subject. No universal bezel, blur, shadow or 3D treatment is supported.
7. Inspect adjacent scenes together at playback speed. A good isolated move can still produce a poor handoff.

### 2. Move between detail and context

**Scene choreography · 5/5 films · 3/3 brands · 29 distinct supported scenes**
